# MenoLisa Upper Body Strength Program
### An 8-Week Progressive Plan for Women 45+

---

## Program Philosophy

This plan keeps your original four goals intact but rebuilds each block around
the 12 exercises on hand. The push-up family (Wall → Table → Bench → Dumbbell
Floor Press) does double duty: **Wall Push-Up** becomes the nervous-system
"wake-up" every single session, while **Table → Bench → Floor Press** becomes
the progressive loading vehicle for the functional push, so a woman with zero
push-up capacity in Week 1 is doing a weighted floor press by Week 7 without
ever feeling like the exercise "changed."

**Frequency:** 2x/week (non-consecutive days), or 3x/week if recovery allows.
**Rest:** 60–90 sec between accessory sets, 90–120 sec between structural sets
(Overhead Press, Row).
**Load cue:** "Heavy" means the last 2 reps of a set feel genuinely hard with
clean form — not a number on the dumbbell. Re-test loadable weight every 2 weeks.

| Block | Goal (unchanged from original plan) | What fills it here |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Prep | Wake up the nervous system, lubricate joints | Wall Push-Up |
| 2. Structural | Bone density + foundational upper-body power | Overhead Press + Bent-Over Row |
| 3. Functional | Stability, multi-directional strength, anti-rotation | Push-Up progression + Single-Arm Row |
| 4. Accessory | Isolate small muscles, protect shoulders/elbows/posture | Lateral Raise, Rear-Delt Fly, Y-T-W, Triceps Extension |

---

## Phase 1 · Weeks 1–2 — Foundation
*Goal: groove the patterns, build confidence, light-to-moderate load.*

**Block 1 — Prep**
- Wall Push-Up — 2 sets x 10 reps

**Block 2 — Structural (Heavy Load)**
- Seated Overhead Press — 3 sets x 8 reps *(seated removes leg drive/core cheat — pure shoulder strength check)*
- Bent-Over Dumbbell Row — 3 sets x 10 reps *(pull weights up in 1 count, lower on a slow 3-count)*

**Block 3 — Functional Push & Pull**
- Table Push-Up — 2 sets x 8 reps
- Single-Arm Dumbbell Row — 2 sets x 10 reps/side *(brace the core — resist the urge to rotate toward the working arm)*

**Block 4 — Accessory / Arm Armor**
- Dumbbell Lateral Raise — 2 sets x 10 reps
- Rear-Delt Fly — 2 sets x 10 reps
- Seated Overhead Triceps Extension — 2 sets x 10 reps

---

## Phase 2 · Weeks 3–4 — Build
*Goal: add volume before adding difficulty. Same exercises, more work capacity.*

**Block 1 — Prep**
- Wall Push-Up — 2 sets x 10–12 reps

**Block 2 — Structural (Heavy Load)**
- Seated Overhead Press — 4 sets x 6 reps *(increase load, drop reps slightly)*
- Bent-Over Dumbbell Row — 4 sets x 10 reps

**Block 3 — Functional Push & Pull**
- Table Push-Up — 3 sets x 10 reps
- Single-Arm Dumbbell Row — 3 sets x 10 reps/side

**Block 4 — Accessory / Arm Armor**
- Dumbbell Lateral Raise — 3 sets x 10 reps
- Y-T-W Shoulder Raise — 2 sets x 8 reps per letter
- Seated Overhead Triceps Extension — 3 sets x 10 reps

---

## Phase 3 · Weeks 5–6 — Progress
*Goal: introduce standing overhead press (bigger core/stability demand) and step the push-up onto the bench.*

**Block 1 — Prep**
- Wall Push-Up — 2 sets x 12 reps

**Block 2 — Structural (Heavy Load)**
- Standing Overhead Press — 3 sets x 6 reps *(new stability challenge — drop the load back down slightly the first week to relearn it standing)*
- Bent-Over Dumbbell Row — 4 sets x 10 reps, heavier than Phase 2

**Block 3 — Functional Push & Pull**
- Bench Push-Up — 3 sets x 10 reps
- Single-Arm Dumbbell Row — 3 sets x 10 reps/side, heavier

**Block 4 — Accessory / Arm Armor**
- Dumbbell Lateral Raise — 3 sets x 12 reps
- Rear-Delt Fly — 3 sets x 10 reps
- Y-T-W Shoulder Raise — 2 sets x 8 reps per letter
- Seated Overhead Triceps Extension — 3 sets x 10 reps

---

## Phase 4 · Weeks 7–8 — Peak
*Goal: heaviest structural load of the program; push-up progression graduates to a fully loaded floor press.*

**Block 1 — Prep**
- Wall Push-Up — 2 sets x 12 reps

**Block 2 — Structural (Heavy Load)**
- Standing Overhead Press — 4 sets x 4–5 reps *(this is your true heavy-load bone-stimulus set — matches the original plan's 4x4 intent)*
- Bent-Over Dumbbell Row — 4 sets x 8 reps, heaviest load of the program

**Block 3 — Functional Push & Pull**
- Dumbbell Floor Press — 3 sets x 10 reps *(the push-up progression's final step — full external load, floor-protected shoulders)*
- Single-Arm Dumbbell Row — 3 sets x 10 reps/side, heaviest + slower 3-count lowering

**Block 4 — Accessory / Arm Armor**
- Dumbbell Lateral Raise — 3 sets x 12 reps
- Y-T-W Shoulder Raise — 3 sets x 8 reps per letter
- Rear-Delt Fly — 3 sets x 10 reps
- Seated Overhead Triceps Extension — 3 sets x 12 reps

---

## Progression Logic at a Glance

| Exercise | Wk 1–2 | Wk 3–4 | Wk 5–6 | Wk 7–8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Push (Block 3) | Table 2x8 | Table 3x10 | Bench 3x10 | Floor Press 3x10 |
| Overhead Press (Block 2) | Seated 3x8 | Seated 4x6 | Standing 3x6 | Standing 4x4-5 |
| Bent-Over Row (Block 2) | 3x10 | 4x10 | 4x10 (heavier) | 4x8 (heaviest) |
| Single-Arm Row (Block 3) | 2x10/side | 3x10/side | 3x10/side (heavier) | 3x10/side (heaviest) |
| Lateral Raise | 2x10 | 3x10 | 3x12 | 3x12 |
| Rear-Delt Fly / Y-T-W | rotate weekly | rotate weekly | both, 2-3 sets | both, 3 sets |
| Triceps Extension | 2x10 | 3x10 | 3x10 | 3x12 |

**Why this order:** load (Block 2) always goes before instability (Block 3) —
this protects the shoulder joint by grooving the strength pattern before
asking the joint to stabilize through a harder lever arm. The push-up ladder
and the seated-to-standing press are the two "invisible" progressions that
make an 8-week beginner plan feel advanced by the end without ever swapping
in an unfamiliar movement.

## Notes for App Implementation
- Each week can reuse the same video/demo assets as its phase — only sets/reps/exercise-variant text needs to update, which keeps content production light.
- Consider a simple in-app toggle "harder / easier" on Block 2 and 3 exercises for women who need to repeat a phase before progressing (common in this age group post-injury or with lower baseline grip/shoulder strength).
- A short form cue per exercise (e.g., row tempo, anti-rotation bracing on single-arm row) would work well as an overlay tooltip or short Lisa voiceover line.
